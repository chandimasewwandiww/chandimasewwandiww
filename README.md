### Lightweight Phishing Email Detection for SMEs

 

Small businesses can't always afford heavy AI models, so I tested whether simple ones could do the job. I compared Naïve Bayes, Logistic Regression and a fine-tuned DistilBERT model. Logistic Regression came within 0.5 F1 points of DistilBERT, trained about five times faster on an ordinary CPU, and caught 88.5% of AI-generated phishing emails, against 32.1% for DistilBERT.
